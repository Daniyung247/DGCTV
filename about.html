About DGC TV

DGC TV is a Nigerian digital media platform bringing together news, entertainment, sports, education, jobs, culture and useful stories in one place. Our goal is to give readers a clear, fast and trustworthy daily source of information.

What we publish

We publish original summaries and reports, and we curate stories from public reporting and official sources. Where a story is based on another outlet's reporting, we aim to say so and to link or point readers to the original source.

Editorial standards

We verify information before publishing, separate fact from opinion, attribute our sources, respect people's privacy, and correct material errors openly and promptly.

Corrections

If you spot an error or want a story updated, email us at daniyunggraphics@gmail.com with the headline and the correction. We review every request.

Advertising

DGC TV may display advertising, including Google AdSense. Advertising does not influence our editorial decisions.
